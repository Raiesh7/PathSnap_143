THE JOURNEY EXPLAINED

From noisy points to a connected path.

  1. Choose a journey

    A trip contains a sequence of GPS observations and a road network.

  2. Score the candidates

    The classifier considers distance, direction, movement and local context.

  3. Connect the sequence

    HMM / Viterbi balances candidate scores with plausible movement between roads.

This interface currently simulates that flow with synthetic fixtures. No model is executed, no evaluator files are loaded, and displayed metrics are illustrative.
